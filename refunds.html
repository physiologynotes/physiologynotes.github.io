Cancellation & Refunds Policy

Last updated: 5 June 2026

Our products are digital study notes that are delivered electronically. Because a digital file cannot be "returned" once it has been delivered, the following policy applies.

Cancellations

Orders cannot be cancelled once payment is complete and the notes have been delivered, due to the instant nature of digital products.

Refunds

As a general rule, all sales are final and no refunds are issued once the notes have been delivered. However, we will provide a full refund or correction in the following cases:

  • You were charged but did not receive your notes, and we are unable to deliver them to you.
  • You were charged more than once for the same order (duplicate payment).
  • You received the wrong product.

How to request a refund

Email us at swayonbhunia121@gmail.com within 7 days of your purchase, including your payment ID and a description of the issue. Approved refunds are processed back to your original payment method through Razorpay, typically within 5–7 business days.

Note: This reflects a standard "no refund once delivered, except for delivery/billing errors" policy. If you'd prefer a more generous window (for example, a short no-questions-asked period), this section can be adjusted.
