Privacy Policy

Last updated: 5 June 2026

This policy explains what information Physiology RECAP collects, how we use it, and your choices. We respect your privacy and collect only what we need to deliver your notes and run the service.

1. Information we collect

  • Contact details — your name and email address, provided at checkout or when you subscribe to updates.
  • Payment information — processed directly by Razorpay. We do not see or store your card or banking details.
  • Basic usage data — standard technical information your browser sends when visiting the site.

2. How we use your information

  • To deliver the notes you purchase to your email address.
  • To respond to your support queries.
  • To send updates about new notes, only if you have subscribed.

3. Payment processing

Payments are handled by Razorpay, which processes your payment information under its own privacy policy and security standards. We receive only confirmation of your payment and the contact details needed to deliver your order.

4. Third-party services

The site uses Razorpay (payments), an email/newsletter service for updates, and embedded YouTube videos (provided by Google). These services may set their own cookies or collect data under their respective privacy policies.

5. Cookies

We keep cookie use to a minimum. Some third-party content embedded on the site, such as YouTube videos, may set cookies on your device.

6. Sharing of data

We do not sell your personal information. We share it only with the service providers needed to process payments and deliver your order.

7. Data retention & your rights

We retain purchase and contact records for as long as needed for our records and legal obligations. You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal data by emailing us.

8. Children

Our materials are intended for students; if you are a minor, please use the site and make purchases with the involvement of a parent or guardian.

9. Changes & contact

We may update this policy from time to time. For any privacy questions or requests, contact us at swayonbhunia121@gmail.com.
